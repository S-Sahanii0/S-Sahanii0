## 🧭 About me

I've spent 5 years building apps, mostly with Flutter, plus some Django on the backend. Now I'm doing a Master's in Computing (Research) at Multimedia University, where I work on making AI fairer. Based in Malaysia, originally from Nepal.

## 🔬 What I'm working on

**My thesis:** *Mitigating Socioeconomic Bias in ICU Mortality Prediction: A Fairness-Aware and Explainable Approach Using SHAP*

In plain words: a hospital model shouldn't be quietly worse for people just because of their income or background. I'm testing how to catch that and fix it without ruining the model.

Tools: MIMIC-IV, eICU, XGBoost, SHAP, Fairlearn.
